About Endeavour

Founder's Story

"Namaste. I hold a PhD in Education Policy from Harvard University, and for the last 17 years I worked in the United States, studying why some schools lift every child and others quietly leave children behind. I advised school districts, trained teachers and published research. By any measure it was a good career."

"But every time I came home to India, I met bright children with real talent and no place that knew how to grow it. I kept asking myself one question: what is the use of knowing how great schools work if I never build one for my own people?"

"So I came back. Endeavour International School will teach students from Grade 1 to Grade 12, in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh. We are affiliated with the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE), and our first batch begins in the coming academic session."

— Dr. Harshvardhan Shrivastva, Founder and Principal

Our Vision

To cultivate inquiring, knowledgeable, and compassionate young leaders who create positive change in society through academic excellence and strong ethical grounding.

Our Mission

To provide an inspiring, child-centric learning environment with low class sizes, expert faculty, and holistic co-curricular development that nurtures every child's unique potential.
